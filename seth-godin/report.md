# Seth Godin: voice study

**A useful read, with gaps.** The test rewrite keeps every fact and adds nothing, and it opens the way his posts do. It picks up only some of his habits, and the closing and the turn did not clearly match what the profile asks for.

Built from 11 posts on seths.blog, November 10 to 19, 2026. 18 rules kept, 9 cut.

## What it learned
- **He argues that easy-to-measure signals are suspect.** He asks what the thing is for before collecting more data: "If it’s easy to measure, it might not be the thing you ought to be measuring." ([post](https://seths.blog/2026/11/dancing-with-the-trophy-shortage/))
- **Posts end on a plain takeaway, often a definition.** 9 of 11 close with a short claim and no recap or question: "Marketing is the simple and brave act of going to where someone else might be, and helping them get to where they hope to go." ([post](https://seths.blog/2026/11/practical-empathy/))
- **The turn gets its own short paragraph.** 7 of 11 posts put the turn in a one-sentence paragraph opening with a word like And, But or Of course: "Of course, this is absurd." ([post](https://seths.blog/2026/11/confusion-about-causation/))
- **Claims are hedged, not shouted.** 9 of 11 posts soften an absolute with words like might or probably: "In fact, it probably isn’t." ([post](https://seths.blog/2026/11/dancing-with-the-trophy-shortage/))

## The test
The same bland company announcement every module gets, rewritten by following the new profile.

**Before**
> We are pleased to announce an update to our quarterly planning process. Going forward, all teams will submit their priorities through a shared template by the second week of each quarter. This change is designed to improve visibility across departments and ensure that resources are allocated effectively. Leadership will review submissions and share a consolidated plan within ten business days. We understand that change can take time to adjust to, and we appreciate everyone's flexibility. If you have questions or feedback about the new process, please reach out to your manager or the operations team. Thank you for your continued commitment to our shared goals.

**After**
> Our quarterly planning process is changing.
>
> Every team will submit its priorities through a shared template by the second week of each quarter. Leadership will review the submissions and share a consolidated plan within ten business days.
>
> Of course, change can take time to adjust to.
>
> If you have questions, ask your manager or the operations team.

It opens on a plain statement, uses short paragraphs, gives the concession its own "Of course," paragraph and has no em dash, which fits his posts. The ending is an instruction about who to ask rather than a takeaway, and the paragraph has no real argument to hedge or turn, so those habits did not show. Every fact survived and nothing was added.

## What's thin
- Several of his argument habits rest on two or three posts each, including the going-to-the-other-person idea, the process-over-launch idea, the numbered list and the repeated title phrase.
- All 11 posts are from within ten days in November 2026, so seasonal and AI topics are over-represented. Older posts would help.
- Only the blog is covered. Nothing is known about LinkedIn, X, Instagram or email.
- Two of the 11 posts are promotions and carry little of his argument voice.
