# Seth Godin: excerpts
Ground truth for the Seth Godin voice study.

## E1 · S2 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/one-swell-foop-2/
> Pay the money, grit your teeth, launch with verve and then move on.

Demonstrates: a three-item series with no comma before "and" (R12).

## E2 · S5 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/confusion-about-causation/
> Of course, this is absurd.

Demonstrates: a one-sentence paragraph that concedes and turns, opening with "Of course," (R10, R15).

## E3 · S1 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/dancing-with-the-trophy-shortage/
> It’s easy to get distracted by (and hooked on) the quest for a trophy. It’s easy to measure and a bright light to focus on.

Demonstrates: a statement opener with a parenthetical aside inside the sentence (R4, R13).

## E4 · S1 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/dancing-with-the-trophy-shortage/
> If it’s easy to measure, it might not be the thing you ought to be measuring. In fact, it probably isn’t.

Demonstrates: a conditional closing line with hedges (R1, R5, R11).

## E5 · S5 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/confusion-about-causation/
> If you want to know why your marketing, your investments or your arguments are floundering, it might pay to reconsider the theory, not look for more data to justify the current one.

Demonstrates: a conditional ending, a series with no serial comma, and a hedge (R1, R5, R11, R12).

## E6 · S9 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/learning-from-rejection/
> More than 400,000 passed my table, and I sold four books.

Demonstrates: a specific, countable example in place of an abstract claim (R7).

## E7 · S9 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/learning-from-rejection/
> Learning from rejection begins with understanding the lesson that’s being offered.

Demonstrates: a last line that repeats the title's phrase (R5, R6).

## E8 · S7 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/ten-minutes-a-day/
> There are two hard parts:

Demonstrates: a colon sentence naming the count before a numbered list (R8).

## E9 · S4 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/practical-empathy/
> Marketing is the simple and brave act of going to where someone else might be, and helping them get to where they hope to go.

Demonstrates: a closing definition as the takeaway, and the going-to-them conviction (R2, R5).

## E10 · S4 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/practical-empathy/
> And so, our only opportunity is to go where they are, not to demand that they come to us.

Demonstrates: a paragraph that opens with And, plus "we/our" for the shared habit (R2, R10, R16).

## E11 · S3 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/selling-to-friends/
> If you’re asking for a favor, you’re not serving and you’re not selling. You’re trading.

Demonstrates: "you" for the reader's choice, and a short reframing sentence (R16).

## E12 · S6 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/have-your-ai-call-my-ai/
> It’s called the fine print for a reason–you’re not supposed to read it. But you should.

Demonstrates: an unspaced en dash with no em dash, and a short turn opening with But (R10, R14).

## E13 · S2 · blog · https://seths.blog/2026/11/one-swell-foop-2/
> The successful path is a process, an iterative, evolving, persistent path toward better.

Demonstrates: a closing claim about process over one-off effort (R3, R5).
